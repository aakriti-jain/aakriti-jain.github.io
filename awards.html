Awards and Honors
  • Michael E. Conrad Graduate Research Award, Runner-Up, 2025 🏆 🎉
    Department of Computer Science, Wayne State University, USA (announced April 14, 2025)
    Awarded for our first-author paper “Burst-MAC: A MAC Protocol for Handling Burst Traffic in LoRa Network” (IEEE RTSS 2024). The Graduate Committee selected the paper from many exceptional submissions for its innovative methodology and meaningful impact on the field of computer science, recognizing it as an advance in the understanding of real-time and networked systems. The award honors research that reflects the pioneering spirit and intellectual boldness championed by Dr. Michael Conrad.
